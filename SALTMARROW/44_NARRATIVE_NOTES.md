# SALTMARROW: PART 44, NARRATIVE: THE NOTES

The full text of the 64 notes in v1. Each entry: ID, title, style (part 32.15: Typewriter, Handwritten, LANTERN, Child, Printed), location (fixed POI or a random pool), and the text. Notes are the main way the story reaches players, so the writing matters: short, specific, human, and never explaining too much. Consistency rules are in part 02.4.1. No em dashes anywhere (part 02).

Fixed-location notes always spawn in their place. Pool notes spawn from the district pool at random spawn points (2 to 4 per district per raid). A player who's read a note can still find it again; the Journal marks it as read.

---

## 44.9 WRITING NEW NOTES

When you add notes (and you will, for new POIs and contracts), follow the voice: specific names and times, ordinary people noticing something wrong, never more than one revelation per note, and leave the reader to connect it. Read N01, N11, N29 and N50 before writing anything new: they're the tonal anchors for the four voices (official log, personal letter, institutional record, LANTERN).

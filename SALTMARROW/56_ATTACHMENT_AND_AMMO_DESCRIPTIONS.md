# SALTMARROW: PART 56, ATTACHMENT AND AMMO DESCRIPTIONS

Every attachment and ammo type needs a description in the inspect panel, in the world's voice (part 02.11), under 40 words, with the mechanical stats shown separately. Here are the descriptions for the catalog in part 09 and the ammo in part 05, organized the same way. Where a family has many near-identical parts (rail sections, magazines), write one description per family member using the same pattern. Tomasz is the implied voice of most gun parts: practical, opinionated and slightly in love with machining.

---

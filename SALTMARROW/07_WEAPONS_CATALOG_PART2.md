# SALTMARROW: PART 07, WEAPONS CATALOG, PART 2 (BATTLE RIFLES, DMRS, BOLT ACTIONS, SHOTGUNS, LMGS, SPECIALS)

The format is the same as part 06. This part covers the heavier end of the arsenal: weapons that reward patience, range and planning, plus the oddities that give the setting its personality.

---

## 7.1 BATTLE RIFLES

Full-power 7.62x51 rifles. Heavier, louder and harder to control than assault rifles, and they kill through armor that shrugs off intermediate rounds. A battle rifle in a good build is the "I came here to win" weapon.

## 7.8 WEAPON SOURCING AND MODELING STRATEGY

Thirty-six weapons is a lot of 3D art. Here's the honest plan, because you can't sculpt them all from scratch, and placeholder cubes would kill Pillar 2.

First, look on Fab for free and permissively licensed weapon packs that suit the style (realistic, modular). Check every license and record it in `CREDITS.md`. Modular kits (separate receiver, barrel, handguard, stock, etc.) are gold for us because the gunsmith needs separate parts anyway.

Second, for anything not covered, build weapons as modular kitbashes in-engine from simple shapes using the Modeling Tools (Modeling Mode in the editor), with good materials doing the heavy lifting: a well-shaded box with a bevel and a great weathered-metal material reads surprisingly well at third-person distance. Prioritize the first-person ADS view: what you see when you aim (the rear of the receiver, the optic, the top of the handguard, the front sight) needs the most detail.

Third, procedural detail: use decals for engravings, logos, serial numbers and wear, rather than modeling them. Use the master weapon material from part 26, which gets edge wear from a curvature map, salt crust from a world-space mask and cosmetic skins from a texture set.

Fourth, the parts are more important than the base gun. Every attachment needs a mesh. Prioritize the Mako's parts (it's the showcase), then the most common parts across weapons (optics, muzzle devices, grips, lights), because those are shared.

If any weapon has to ship as a placeholder in a tier, it gets a clear "WIP" tag in dev builds and a decent kitbashed silhouette, never a grey box.

# SALTMARROW: PART 49, THE EAST END, THE MOOR AND THE ISLETS, ROOM BY ROOM

The rest of the island's hero locations, in the format of parts 47 and 48. These are Tier 2 content. Build the blockouts with the rest of the island in Milestone 6 and bring them to this level of detail district by district.

---

## 49.1 ST. COLUMBA'S COTTAGE HOSPITAL

Building origin: world (-55, +315, 4.0). An L-shaped two-storey building: the main wing 14 × 40 m (east-west), the ward wing 14 × 30 m (north-south) at the east end. Basement under the main wing (Z -3.2). Floors 3.4 m. Rendered walls painted pale green, a flat roof with a helipad marking (faded) on the ward wing.

Flood: basement Surge 1 (fully), ground floor Surge 3 (0.8 m).

### 49.1.1 Ground floor

Reception and Waiting (10 × 12 m): a desk, rows of chairs, leaflets, a children's corner, a donations box (Scrip in coins). Containers: desk [LT_Desk_Reception], donations box [LT_CashBox].

A&E (12 × 10 m): three curtained bays (beds, monitors, crash trolley [LT_CrashTrolley: IFAK, tourniquets, adrenaline shot 10%]), a resus room (a defibrillator: an interactable that "shocks" anyone in front of it for 20 damage, a joke and a trap), the nurses' base. AI: 2 Shamblers on trolleys (sit slumped variant: lying).

Pharmacy (6 × 6 m, locked, Pharmacy Key from the matron's office): shelves of medicines [LT_Pharmacy_Hospital: the best medical loot on the map: surgical kits 15%, morphine, antibiotics, painkillers, hemostatics, stimulants 8%].

X-Ray (6 × 8 m): the machine, lead aprons (barter: Steel Plate equivalent "Lead Sheet"), a lightbox with X-rays of a hand with crystals growing in the joints (a story prop).

Outpatients (corridor of 4 consulting rooms): desks [LT_Desk_Medical], examination couches, cabinets [LT_Medical_Cabinet].

Kitchen (8 × 8 m): commercial kitchen [LT_Kitchen_Commercial].

### 49.1.2 First floor

Men's Ward and Women's Ward (each 12 × 14 m, 8 beds): beds with patients' belongings in bedside lockers [LT_Bedside_Hospital], drip stands, curtains (soft cover), the barricaded doors (part 02: chairs and beds stacked against the doors from inside; breaking the barricade takes 6 melee hits or one explosive). AI: 4 Shamblers in hospital gowns in each ward (lying in beds: they sit up when disturbed).

The Children's Ward (10 × 10 m, 6 small beds): drawings on the walls, a mural of a whale, toys, and the one bed made up neatly with fresh heather in a vase and the card (N31). No AI here, ever. The quietest room on the island. Light it with a single window and let the rain sound in.

The Nurses' Station (central, 6 × 4 m): the logbook (N29), a drugs trolley [LT_DrugsTrolley], phones, a rota board.

The Matron's Office (5 × 5 m): the order (N30), the Pharmacy Key (desk drawer) [LT_Desk_Matron], a filing cabinet with patient records (barter paper).

The Operating Theater (8 × 8 m): a table, lights, an anesthesia machine, instrument trays [LT_Theater: surgical kit 20%, field surgery pouch], a scrub room.

### 49.1.3 Basement

The Cold Room (4 × 6 m): the medical refrigerator (the insulin, CAP02; also general medical [LT_ColdRoom]), the seal on the door (if the basement is flooded, you dive to reach it: swim 15 m underwater along the corridor, part 04.7.4: a breath-holding challenge with a 30 s budget, the corridor has one air pocket halfway at a ceiling void).

The Morgue (6 × 8 m): steel drawers (8: 4 with Hollows that slide out when a player enters: a scripted scare, and they're Crawlers), slabs, a desk with a log. Containers: desk.

The Boiler Room and the Generator Room: barter (copper, motors, a car battery), the hospital's emergency generator (an interaction: start it with fuel to power the lights for 5 minutes; a light-and-noise event).

## 49.2 THE PRIMARY SCHOOL

Building origin: world (55, +250, 9.0). Single storey, 12 × 30 m, with a hall wing (12 × 12 m) and a playground (fenced, 30 × 20 m: a climbing frame, a hopscotch grid, a bench).

Rooms: Classroom 1 (P1 to P3: small chairs, a story corner, a nature table with shells and sea glass), Classroom 2 (P4 to P7: desks, the weather diary N32, a map of the island the children drew, a fish tank (empty, dry)), the Hall (a stage with a nativity play's props in a box, PE equipment, folded tables), the Head's Office (a desk, a phone, a framed photo of the whole school (34 children) on the wall), the Kitchen (small, food). Containers: 14 total [LT_School: food, barter, notes pool, nothing valuable]. AI: none at raid start (part 14.7.3: keep it quiet). Hollows can wander in during Surges.

The school is a breathing space. It has no combat design, no hot loot, and one of the strongest emotional beats in the game. Don't change that.

## 49.3 THE EAST END TERRACES

Four terraces of 12 houses (part 14.7.2): Harbour Terrace, Kirk Holm View, Cannery Row, Shore Street. Built from the terrace archetypes with PCG interior fill. Specific houses worth hand-dressing (one per terrace): No. 4 Harbour Terrace (a fisherman's family: a boat model, oilskins, a family photo with the Bonnie Jean, N34 on the fridge), No. 9 Kirk Holm View (an old woman's house: a budgie cage (empty), knitting, a chair facing the window toward Kirk Holm, binoculars, a notebook logging "lights on the Holm" for nine years: a story twist: someone's been living here after the evacuation; the last entry is a month ago), No. 2 Cannery Row (a cannery worker's: overalls, union badges, a dartboard), No. 11 Shore Street (flooded in every Surge: salt-crusted to the ceiling, ruined, with the best salt deposits in the East End).

Back yards: sheds [LT_Shed], coal bunkers, washing lines, outside toilets, bicycles. The lanes between terraces: narrow, with walls to mantle.

The Allotments (40 × 30 m, east of the terraces): plots with sheds (8 [LT_Shed_Allotment: tools, seeds (N35), fuel]), a polytunnel (torn), compost bins, a scarecrow (it looks like a person at a distance at night: a fun false alarm).

## 49.4 THE POWER STATION (WARDEN STRONGHOLD)

Compound origin: world (285, -470, 22.0). A fenced compound 150 × 100 m: a 2.4 m chain-link fence with barbed wire (climbable only at two damaged sections; otherwise through the gates or by cutting: no cutters in v1, so through the gaps), a main gate with a gatehouse (2 Wardens), a rear gate (chained).

Generator Halls ×3 (each 30 × 15 m, 10 m tall): huge diesel engines on plinths (full cover), gantries along the walls at 5 m (reached by ladders), control panels, exhaust pipes, an overhead crane. Two halls run (deafening noise: footstep radii inside are halved because nobody can hear anything over the engines, part 28), one is shut down ("Generator three's shaking again"). Containers: per hall, 2 tool cabinets [LT_Industrial_Tools], spare parts crates [LT_Crate_Engine].

The Control Room Building (20 × 12 m, two floors): ground: the switchgear room, the workshop (the Armorer's Tool Roll for TOM08 [LT_Workshop_Warden]), the store; upper: the control room (consoles, the patrol board N36, the fuse cabinet with Slipway Fuses [LT_FuseCabinet]), the Warden officer's office (a desk with maps, a radio, keys, [LT_Desk_Officer: the LANTERN Keycard 10%, Military Encrypted Tablet 15%]). The roof: the Grom position behind sandbags (part 07, TOM13), with a view over the compound and down the peat road (400 m).

The Fuel Tank Farm (40 × 30 m, inside a bunded wall 1.2 m high): six tanks (each 8 m diameter, 8 m tall, with ladders), pipework, a pump house. Tanks can be shot to leak (each takes 300 damage to puncture; a puncture spills burning or flammable diesel if ignited by incendiary rounds, a flare or an explosion: a massive fire event that burns for 3 minutes and lights the whole moor; it damages everything inside the bund and drives Wardens out). The pump house has fuel cans (NIL10).

The Barracks (a prefab block, 20 × 8 m): bunk rooms (4, [LT_Bunk_Warden: Warden gear, rations, letters N37]), a mess, an armory locker (locked, officer's key: [LT_WardenArmory: Bora, Bora-S, ammo, grenades, Class 4 armor 20%]), showers.

AI: 6 to 9 Wardens at raid start (one officer): 2 at the gate, 1 on the Grom, 2 patrolling the compound, 1 to 2 in the control room, 1 to 2 in the barracks. Reinforcements from the peat road if the alarm is raised (a siren on the control room roof: if any Warden reaches the siren switch while alerted, it sounds and calls reinforcements in 90 s).

Approaches: the main gate (frontal, covered by the Grom), the fence gaps (the north side, facing the moor: a long crawl through heather), the tank farm side (west: the tanks as cover, and a very bad place to be shot at with incendiaries), the wind farm ridge (northeast: high ground with a 300 m view into the compound for snipers).

## 49.5 THE AIRFIELD

Origin: world (180, -590, 30.0). The runway (600 m × 30 m tarmac, running east-west, with faded markings and a windsock), the terminal hut (a tiny building with a waiting room, an office (the flight log N39) and a radio), the hangar (30 × 25 m: the wrecked light aircraft (a container in its cabin), the air ambulance helicopter (intact, unflyable: a container in its cabin [LT_Helicopter: medical, a flare gun]), tool racks, a mezzanine office), the fuel bowser (a truck: shootable, flammable), the fire tender garage (a fire engine: containers in its lockers [LT_FireEngine: axe (set dressing), hoses, first aid, breathing apparatus (barter)]).

The Helipad (E9): a painted pad next to the hangar with landing lights (dead; the flare marks it).

The airfield is a wide-open space: the runway is a 600 m sightline, and crossing it is dangerous. Cover: the hangar, the bowser, the terminal hut, a few abandoned vehicles, the runway's drainage ditches (0.8 m deep: crouch-walk cover).

## 49.6 THE SALTMARROW DISTILLERY

Origin: world (420, +180, 18.0). The north coast. Buildings: the Still House (20 × 14 m, 12 m tall: four copper pot stills, gleaming under salt-dust, catwalks around them at 4 m, the spirit safe (a brass and glass box: decorative, shootable), pipework), the Mash House (16 × 12 m: a mash tun, washbacks (big wooden vats: you can fall in one; they're empty, 3 m deep: a trap and a hiding place)), Warehouses ×2 (each 30 × 15 m: racks of casks three high (wooden: penetrable, and shooting a cask spills whisky with a lovely splash and a smell VFX), dark, narrow aisles: close quarters), the Visitor Centre and Shop (a tasting bar, a shop with bottles (valuables: the 12 Year), tourist trinkets, a cafe), the Manager's Office (in the visitor centre's upper floor: the cask ledger N38, the safe with the Founder's Cask [LT_Safe_Distillery: Saltmarrow 25 Year, Scrip], a window over the pier), the Pier (E6 extraction: a stone pier where the distillery's boats loaded casks, 50 m long, with a crane).

Fire hazard: the warehouses and the still house are full of spirit: incendiary rounds or explosions set them burning (a huge fire event; the warehouses burn for the rest of the raid, lighting the north coast).

AI: 4 Shamblers (2 in the still house, 1 in a warehouse, 1 at the tasting bar: memory point "tasting"), 2 Crawlers in the washbacks.

## 49.7 THE CARAVAN PARK

Origin: world (470, -150, 20.0). 40 static caravans in 5 rows on a grassy clifftop (each caravan 3 × 10 m: a lounge, a kitchen, two bedrooms, a toilet; 4 to 6 containers each [LT_Caravan: low tier]), a shower block, the site shop (food, gas cylinders, maps), a playground, a reception hut, the clifftop path down to the cove (E7: a dinghy on the beach), and the barricaded caravan (row 5, end: boarded windows, a padlocked door with a recent padlock, a rain-collection barrel outside, inside: a lived-in space with bedding, tins, a radio, a diary (N33), a photograph of Old Cormac and a younger man, and a rifle rack (empty): the survivor is out, and their identity is a future seed). The zip line anchor to the Teeth (Tier 2: part 14.9.3) is at the cliff edge beside row 1.

AI: 6 Shamblers spread through the rows (low density: it's the new-player-friendly area).

## 49.8 THE WATER WORKS, THE WIND FARM, THE GOLF CLUBHOUSE AND THE CROFTS

The Water Treatment Works (origin (340, +20, 46.0)): two settling tanks (round, 15 m diameter, open, full of dark water: falling in is swimming), a filter house (filters: Water Filter items for CAP03 [LT_Filters]), the pump room (CAP03's visit objective; pipes, valves, a control panel), the lab (N42, sample bottles, [LT_Lab]).

The Wind Farm (6 turbines along the ridge between (380, -300) and (220, -100), elevation 40 to 50 m): each turbine base hut (a steel door, a control cabinet [LT_Electrical], a ladder inside the tower: 60 m climb to the nacelle at 1.3 m/s, about 46 s, totally exposed if someone below looks up; the nacelle has a hatch to the roof: the highest point on the island except the church spire, with a 360° view, and a lightning magnet). One turbine has collapsed (its tower lies across the peat road: cover and a landmark).

The Golf Clubhouse (origin (250, +120, 38.0)): a bar, a lounge with honours boards, the pro shop (golf clubs as set dressing, waterproofs: barter fabric), the notice (N41), the greenkeeper's shed (a mower, fuel). Tier 2.

The Crofts (6 farmhouses, each a 1.5-storey stone cottage with a byre (barn) and a stone-walled yard): kitchens, box beds, peat stacks, sheep pens, a tractor. Tier 1 to 2. One croft has the diary (N40). Sheep wander the moor around them (part 27.8).

## 49.9 GANNET ROCK

Origin: world (-470, -360). The landing stage at Z 1.0 (a concrete platform with a crane and a ladder), the steps (140 steps cut into the rock, 1.2 m wide with an iron rail, switching back three times: a long climb of about 60 s, exposed to anyone on the summit), the summit at Z 40: the lighthouse (a 22 m tower: the ground floor service room (fuel, tools), the spiral stair (100 steps), the watch room (a desk with Cormac's log, N44), the lamp room (the lens: the Lighthouse Lens Prism item spawns here once per raid; the lamp mechanism; the gallery outside, a 1 m walkway with a railing all round: a 360° sniper position at Z 62 above the sea), the keeper's cottages (two, each 6 × 10 m: Cormac's with the diary N43, a stove, a bed, books on birds, a radio, a rifle rack (the Old Mariner 35%) [LT_KeeperCottage]; the other empty), the fog signal house (N45, the horns, a generator), the helipad (E10).

Gannets everywhere: on every ledge, with their noise and their smell (part 27.8). They burst off the cliffs at gunshots: a visible tell across the harbor.

AI: 2 Shamblers on the landing stage (staring up the steps), none at the summit (Cormac's light keeps them back, Agnes would say).

## 49.10 KIRK HOLM

Origin: world (-20, +560). The jetty on the west side (E11), the causeway arriving from the East End (part 14.7.4).

The Quarantine Hospital (a ruined two-storey stone building around a courtyard, 30 × 30 m): the west wing (roofed: wards with iron bedsteads, a dispensary [LT_Dispensary_Old: old medical, the 1893 register for AGN08], the superintendent's office), the east wing (roofless: walls and window openings, grass growing in the wards, a great place for a firefight in the rain), the courtyard (a well, a dead tree), the mortuary (a low stone building, cold, with stone slabs).

The Graveyard: old leaning stones from the 1800s and new graves from Year 0 (wooden crosses with names painted on), the mass grave with its cairn (the note N47 under a stone).

The Sunken Chapel: a small medieval chapel half collapsed into subsided ground at its east end. The nave is intact (a few pews, a stone altar); the chancel has dropped 2 m into a hole. The crypt below floods at Surge 1, and the water draining from it after each Surge leaves rich salt (the warmest salt on the map: AGN05's sample). At the back of the crypt, an iron gate (locked, no key in v1) with a passage into the rock beyond, and a faint glow and a faint hum coming through it. That's the seed. Don't explain it.

AI: 4 Shamblers in the graveyard (standing at the mass grave), 2 in the hospital, Drowners in the channel between Kirk Holm and the East End after Surge 1.

## 49.11 THE TEETH: LISTENER

Origin: world (590, -80) for the main skerry's center. The skerry is 160 × 90 m of bare rock, up to Z 22, with the compound on a leveled terrace at Z 12.

The Compound: a 3 m fence with razor wire (one gate facing the jetty, one gap blasted in the north side), the Jetty (Z 2: a concrete berth, a crane, a boathouse), the Barracks (two blocks: bunk rooms, a mess, a gym, lockers [LT_Locker_Military]), the Operations Building (two storeys, 30 × 20 m, with the radome on its roof (a 10 m white sphere: climbable via a maintenance ladder; a lightning target)): ground: the guard room, the comms room (radios, the encrypted tablets [LT_Comms: Military Encrypted Tablet 30%]), the briefing room (a map of the North Atlantic with the trench marked and the word "STRUCTURE" in a box), the armory (locked, officer's key: [LT_ArmoryMilitary: Frigate 5%, Corsair 10%, Albatross 10%, Class 5 armor 10%, AP ammo]); first floor: offices (desks with LANTERN documents: N50, N51, N52 pool), the commander's office (a safe [LT_Safe_Military]), the analysts' room (screens, printouts, coffee cups nine years old). The Generator Shed (diesel generators: they still run on Warden fuel), the Helipad (E13: a painted pad with a windsock and a radio post).

Underground (Tier 2, through a reinforced stairwell in the operations building's core, past a blast door opened by the LANTERN Keycard): Level B1, the LANTERN offices (labs with sample cases (empty, broken), a cold storage room with frost, the archive with the hard drive (AGN12) [LT_Archive_LANTERN]), Level B2, the control room (a horseshoe of consoles facing a wall of dead screens and one live terminal showing the array status (N54) and a pulse countdown; the BELLWETHER authorization (N53) in a binder; and the pump room beyond: the Lantern Choir's arena (part 13.5.2): a flooded machine hall with the array's power couplings in the walls, cables grown through with crystal, and the five singers fused in the middle of the room). The water rises during the fight.

AI: 8 to 12 Wardens (2 officers) on the surface, none underground except during the boss fight (the Choir spawns its drowned).

## 49.12 THE TEETH: THE ORLA BRAE

The wreck lies across two skerries 60 m west of LISTENER's skerry, connected to it by a rope bridge the Wardens rigged (a 30 m sagging rope-and-plank bridge: crossing it is slow and exposed; it can be shot: 400 HP, then it falls, and anyone on it drops 8 m into the sea).

The Stern (on the western skerry, Z 0 to 18): the bridge (the captain's cabin with the log N48 and the safe [LT_Safe_Ship: the Ship's Chronometer, Scrip, the Leviathan 3%], the wheelhouse with the chronometer mount and charts, the radio room), the crew quarters (cabins ×8 [LT_Cabin_Ship], a mess, a galley), the engine room (deep, dark, flooding at Surge 1: barter, fuel), the after hold (the cargo: the transducer crates (AGN13), the manifest (N49), and Hollows when the water's up).

The Bow (on the eastern skerry, separated from the stern by a 12 m gap of water where the hull broke: swim it or cross on the fallen mast, which bridges the gap at deck level: a balance-beam route 0.4 m wide that you can walk at walk speed only), the forward hold (flooded to 2 m at low water, fully at Surge 1: Drowners and Crawlers, cargo crates [LT_Cargo_Military]), the forecastle (the anchor windlass, a deck locker).

The ship lists 15° to port. Walking on deck is on a slope; the interior corridors are tilted. Make the movement system handle it (walkable floor angle is 44° by default; the slope is fine, but make sure the camera and the weapon don't feel wrong: no special handling except that props are settled at the tilt and loose items roll to the low side, which is a lovely touch with physics settling at load).

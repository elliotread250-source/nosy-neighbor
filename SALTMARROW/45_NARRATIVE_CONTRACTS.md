# SALTMARROW: PART 45, NARRATIVE: THE CONTRACT LINES

The full contract text for each trader's main line, plus a set of side contracts and the repeatable pools. Each entry gives: ID, title, prerequisites (beyond the previous contract in the line), the trader's offer text (shown on the contract card), objectives, completion line, and rewards (using the bands from part 43.9). The voice is the trader's. Objectives are written as they appear in the UI.

Map contract objectives to the objective types in part 17.3.1 and the data schema in part 32.8.

---

## 45.1 THE CAPTAIN: "KEEPING HER AFLOAT"

### CAP01: Shore Leave
Prereq: none (offered after the Shakedown).
Offer: "You came back. That's the first thing. The second thing is food. The galley's low on tins. Bring me some, and bring yourself back with them."
Objectives: Hand over 4 Found in Raid tinned food items (any). Extract once.
Completion: "Good. The deck crew eats tonight. You'll find I remember who feeds them."
Rewards: 1,200 XP, 15,000 Scrip, Captain +0.03, 2 IFAKs.

### CAP02: Cold Storage
Offer: "The hospital's cold room had three months of insulin in it when the town emptied. If the seal held, it's still good. There's a lad on deck five who won't see Christmas without it. Get into the basement of St. Columba's, find the medical refrigerator, and bring back every vial. Watch the water. That basement floods early."
Objectives: Find the insulin in St. Columba's cold room and extract with it.
Completion: "His mother cried. I nearly did. Don't tell anyone."
Rewards: 2,000 XP, 25,000 Scrip, Captain +0.04, Field Surgery Pouch, unlocks Lifeboat Trauma Kit in stock.

### CAP03: Fresh Water
Offer: "The desalinator's on its last legs. The water works on the moor has filters we can use. They're heavy and they're in a building the Wardens like to walk past. Mind yourself."
Objectives: Hand over 2 Water Filters (Found in Raid). Visit the water treatment works pump room.
Completion: "Clean water for a month. You've done more for this ship this week than half the council."
Rewards: 2,200 XP, 30,000 Scrip, Captain +0.03, Thermos of Tea ×2.

### CAP04: The Bonnie Jean
Offer: "Archie Groat took the Bonnie Jean out to the east shore three days ago to pick up a crew. Neither came back. The boat'll be on the shingle by the noust if the sea's been kind. Find the logbook. I need to know what happened before I tell his wife."
Objectives: Find the Bonnie Jean's wreck on the east shore. Find and extract with the Bonnie Jean's logbook.
Completion: "Hollows in the water off the noust. Archie never saw them. I'll tell Morag it was quick. Maybe it was."
Rewards: 2,600 XP, 35,000 Scrip, Captain +0.04, Signal Pistol with 4 red flares.

### CAP05: Lights for the Lifeboat
Offer: "The lifeboat station still has its spare RIB on the slip. If the winch had power, it'd be a way off the island for anyone caught on the west side. The power station has fuses. The Wardens have the power station. I'm not asking you to start a war. I'm asking you to be clever."
Objectives: Obtain a Slipway Fuse. Install it at the lifeboat station fuse box.
Completion: "That slip's saved more people than I can count. Now it can save a few more."
Rewards: 3,500 XP, 60,000 Scrip, Captain +0.05, unlocks the Lifeboat Slipway extraction info for all your future raids.

### CAP06: Medicine Run
Offer: "Antibiotics, dressings, anything the pharmacy left. Fraser's on the high street had a dispensary. It'll be locked. You'll find a way."
Objectives: Hand over 3 Antibiotics (Found in Raid). Hand over 5 Field Dressings (Found in Raid).
Completion: "Mother Agnes says thank you. She won't say it to your face, she's busy, but she says it."

### CAP07: Someone Else's Stash

### CAP16: Last Boat
Prereq: Captain loyalty 4.
Offer: "I'm told I should retire. I'm told a lot of things. One more job, salvager. The last of the town's records, the council minutes from the night of the Surge, are in the police station's safe. Completion: "They knew. They told us to stay indoors and keep calm, and they knew. Well. Now so do we."
Rewards: 25,000 XP, 600,000 Scrip, Captain +0.15, title: Captain's Favorite, cosmetic set: Ferry Crew (cap, jacket, jumper).
